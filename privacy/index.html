At a glance

  • Your conversations, characters and saved memories are kept in a journal on your device. Selected information is sent to cloud services to provide AI features.
  • Before AI features begin, the app asks for permission to send information to OpenAI through our Google Cloud infrastructure. You can withdraw that permission in Settings.
  • Photos you attach, relevant conversation context and voice recordings used for dictation may be processed by OpenAI. Generated character photos also use appearance references and scene descriptions.
  • Optional iCloud Backup stores an encrypted copy of your workspace with Apple. Billing, analytics and support have separate data flows.
  • Deleting the workspace, closing a wallet account and cancelling an Apple subscription are separate actions. Details and limits are explained below.

Who we are and what this covers

Oddly Alike is provided by Ievgenii Iablonskyi (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy covers the Oddly Alike iPhone app and this website.

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over, with its initial release planned for the United States. For privacy questions and requests, use our support form.

Information we process

What you share in the app

This includes your First Meeting answers, messages, selected photos, characters, appearance choices, preferences, saved memories and the fictional events and scenes associated with your conversations. It may include your chosen name, interests, values, routines, goals, boundaries and the kind of connection you want, when you choose to provide them.

Your messages or attachments may reveal sensitive information, including health, beliefs, relationships or sexuality. Share only what you are comfortable having processed for the feature you use. Avoid sending passwords, financial credentials or another person’s private information without permission.

Photos, camera and microphone

If you attach a photo or use the camera, the app processes the image you select or capture. Attached photos may be included with your message and relevant recent context sent to OpenAI. The app re-encodes attachments rather than intentionally collecting their original location metadata. Places you describe in messages or show in images can still reveal location.

If you use dictation, the app records audio after microphone permission and sends it for transcription. The transcript is returned as editable text. Recording starts when you turn on dictation and may capture nearby sound while active, including if you move the app to the background during recording. Temporary recording files are removed after completion or cancellation in the normal recording flow.

Wallet, purchases and technical information

We process guest or wallet identifiers, authentication and session information, Apple purchase and subscription records, credit balances, usage charges and records needed to restore purchases or prevent duplicate credit grants. Signing in with Apple for the wallet does not request your name or email address through Apple’s name/email permission scopes. This does not make wallet activity anonymous.

Apple handles payment-card details. We receive purchase and entitlement information rather than your full card number. Our services and providers also process information such as app and OS version, device and app-instance identifiers, connection/IP information, request timing, errors and usage measurements.

Support and reports you choose to send

Using the website support form shares your reply email, optional name, selected topic and message with our Google Cloud/Firebase support backend and Resend for delivery to the developer. Connection information and pseudonymous anti-spam identifiers derived from the email and IP address are used to limit abuse. No account is required to submit a request.

Contacting support from the app may share your email address, message and attachments. The app prepares an editable system report with device/app details, language, time zone, usage counts, purchase-plan information and backup status or error categories. You can review and remove that report before sending.

If you report an AI response or image in the app, the selected response or image, your optional comment, and report identifier/time are sent to us through our email-delivery provider. Reports do not automatically include your entire conversation. The selected content itself may contain personal information.

How AI processing works

With your permission, relevant information travels through our Google Cloud/Firebase services to OpenAI to ask interview questions, create a character and starting world, generate replies, select or update memories, plan and generate images, transcribe dictation, and check new text for signals of self-harm risk.

A request may include your current message or answer, selected earlier messages, saved memories, character details, fictional-world context, timestamps or time-zone information, and relevant image attachments. Photo generation also sends the selected character’s reference images and the scene description needed for that image. The app selects relevant excerpts and other context for each request.

Some interview or chat responses can use OpenAI’s web-search tools, including without a separate search command from you. Search queries may be derived from the conversation context and processed by OpenAI and its search-service providers. Using search can therefore share information from that context.

The app asks for agreement before enabling this AI data sharing. To withdraw it, open Settings → My Data → OpenAI Data Sharing and turn it off. AI chat, memory processing, dictation and photo generation pause; saved conversations remain readable. Withdrawal stops subsequent AI requests from the app but cannot recall a request already accepted by a service.

Our Responses API requests ask OpenAI not to save responses as application history. That setting is separate from provider abuse monitoring, temporary prompt caching, other API features and legal retention, and does not guarantee zero retention. OpenAI may retain information under its applicable API terms and data controls. See OpenAI’s API data controls and Privacy Policy.

Why information is used and who receives it

We use information to provide and personalize the features you request; run the service and manage credits and purchases; respond to support or content reports; detect abuse and surface safety resources; understand product performance; and meet legal, security and accounting obligations.

  • OpenAI: AI replies, interview and memory processing, image generation, transcription, relevant image understanding, search and safety classification.
  • Google Cloud and Firebase: application servers, app-integrity checks, storage of wallet and operational records, service logs, and Firebase Analytics. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Apple: App Store purchases and subscriptions, Sign in with Apple, device permission services and optional iCloud/CloudKit backup. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Resend: delivery of website support/privacy requests and selected-content reports you submit in the app. See Resend’s Privacy Policy. Your email provider and ours handle support emails you choose to send.
  • Website hosting and network providers: serving this website and processing ordinary connection and security logs.

We require service providers receiving personal information on our behalf to provide the same or equal protection described in this policy and Apple’s applicable privacy requirements, including appropriate security, limits on use and retention, and support for applicable deletion obligations. Providers also have service-specific agreements and privacy terms. Information may be disclosed when required by law, to address fraud or threats to safety and security, or as reasonably necessary in a business transfer subject to applicable privacy protections.

Analytics, advertising and this website

The public app uses Firebase Analytics from launch. Analytics is separate from the OpenAI permission and is not switched off by withdrawing that permission. It records product interactions and technical information, including interview/chat activity, counts of messages or photos, character appearance selections, dictation duration, subscription/credit status and purchase-product events.

Our custom analytics events do not include message text, interview answers, photos or voice recordings. Analytics providers may also process app-instance/device identifiers, device characteristics, automatic app events and approximate location inferred from connection information. These measurements should not be treated as anonymous simply because they omit your name.

The app does not display third-party advertising. We do not supply conversation text, photos or recordings as advertising audiences. We do not add advertising pixels or analytics cookies to this website. Ordinary hosting and security logs may still include an IP address, browser information and requested page.

This website does not change its behavior in response to browser Do Not Track signals. It has no cross-site tracking feature to switch off. In the app, third-party SDK providers may collect activity information over time and associate it with information from other apps or services under their applicable terms and settings. External sites linked from these pages apply their own privacy practices.

Storage, international processing and security

Your main workspace—including chats, characters, saved memories and photos—is stored on your device. Information selected for an AI or other cloud feature is processed off-device as described above. Wallet and purchase-related information is stored in our cloud infrastructure.

If you turn on iCloud Backup, the app encrypts its workspace backup and supported photo assets before uploading them to your Apple Account’s private CloudKit storage. Recovery uses the app’s recovery-key mechanism and, where available, iCloud Keychain. Keep your recovery key private. This backup protection is separate from AI processing, where providers need to process the selected content.

We use measures including encrypted network connections, app-integrity checks, protected authentication credentials and restricted service access. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Even for a United States release, information may be accessed or processed in other countries by us or our providers. The primary public app API runs in the United States, but that does not mean every provider, support operation, backup or processing step is limited to the United States.

How long information is kept

  • Device journal and backup: kept while you use them, until you remove the relevant data or complete workspace/backup deletion. Copies exported or saved outside the app are controlled separately.
  • AI requests: subject to the provider’s endpoint-specific processing, caching, abuse-monitoring and legal-retention rules. OpenAI’s published defaults list up to 30 days of abuse-monitoring retention for Responses and image edits, subject to exceptions. Its current endpoint table lists no routine abuse-monitoring or application-state retention for transcription and moderation. Actual settings and applicable exceptions can differ; we do not promise immediate deletion of every AI-related copy.
  • Wallet and purchases: kept to operate credits, restore and validate purchases, prevent fraud or duplicate grants, handle disputes and meet applicable legal/accounting requirements. Limited purchase associations and records can remain after wallet closure.
  • Operational and analytics records: kept for service operation, reliability, security, cost/usage measurement and product analysis under the relevant service configuration. Some efficiency records use pseudonymous fingerprints and timing rather than message text. They are separate from the journal and are not automatically erased by deleting a chat.
  • Support and reports: messages and delivery records are kept as needed to address your request or report and any related security, dispute or legal issue, including copies held by email providers.

Retention depends on the purpose and type of record, whether it is still needed for that purpose, requests you make, and legal or security obligations. To ask about or request deletion of information outside the in-app controls, contact us below.

Your choices

  • Review and edit saved memories and the information you choose to share.
  • Turn off OpenAI Data Sharing in the app to pause AI features. This does not close your wallet, stop separate analytics or erase earlier provider copies.
  • Use iPhone settings to manage microphone, camera, photo access and notifications. Character messages and unfinished-interview reminders are scheduled on your device; notification previews can reveal content if you enable them.
  • Choose whether to enable the app’s iCloud Backup. Use the app’s workspace-deletion control when you want its managed backup removed as well.
  • Review a support email and its system report before sending; decide whether to submit a selected-content report.

Deleting your data and wallet

Workspace: open Settings → My Data → Delete All My Data and confirm. This starts removal of the profile, characters, chats, memories and photos in that workspace and its app-managed iCloud backup. Backup deletion may require the same Apple Account and the recovery key. Cloud deletion requires connectivity and can remain pending until the operation completes. Keep the app available to finish a pending deletion.

Wallet account: if you signed in with Apple for the wallet, open Settings → Wallet → Delete Wallet Account. It closes wallet access and revokes the associated sign-in authorization/session access. It does not delete your device journal. A pseudonymous Apple-account association can remain after a purchase or checkout, including a pending approval, for payment reconciliation and restoration of remaining paid credits. Purchase and other records needed for the purposes above may also remain.

Deleting a workspace does not also close the wallet or erase previously collected analytics, provider retention, support communications or required billing records. Closing a wallet does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Manage subscriptions through your Apple Account; see Apple’s cancellation instructions.

Deleting the app alone may leave iCloud backups, protected credentials, provider records and subscriptions. Photos saved to your Photos library, exports and copies on other devices must be managed separately. Contact us for assistance with remaining account, guest, support or service-side records.

Privacy questions and requests

Use the privacy request form to ask about information we handle or request access, correction, deletion or a copy. Depending on your state, the law’s applicability and available exceptions, you may also have rights to restrict certain processing, opt out of specified uses or appeal a decision about a request. We will handle requests as required by applicable law and explain any applicable limitation.

We may need reasonable information to verify a request and locate the relevant records. Do not send your password, full payment-card information or recovery key. We cannot retrieve device-only journal content or decrypt an iCloud backup merely from your email address. If you disagree with our response, reply and request review.

Age, fictional characters and safety

Oddly Alike is intended for adults aged 18 and over. AI characters are fictional, not people. The app displays AI-related disclosures and reminders and warns that AI companions may be unsuitable for some minors.

AI responses can be inaccurate or inappropriate. The app is not a health-care, therapy, crisis-monitoring or emergency-response service. Some new text is checked for self-harm risk to offer support resources; detection is automated and can miss a concern. We do not automatically contact emergency services on your behalf. Read AI and safety for the current protocol and its limits.

If you believe a minor has provided personal information through the app, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.
