AI and safety

Last updated: November 27, 2026

What the current app does, and what automated safety features cannot promise.

A fictional AI character

Oddly Alike generates a fictional character, dialogue, memories and scenes. The character is not a human, does not have human feelings or experiences, and cannot provide a real-world relationship or professional judgment. Responses can be inaccurate, inappropriate or inconsistent.

The app’s introduction explains its AI nature and warns that AI companions may be unsuitable for some minors. In chat, a notice identifies the partner as AI when the reminder is active. Continued chat use triggers periodic reminders. These notices do not make the generated content reliable or clinically safe.

The intended audience is adults aged 18 and over.

Current self-harm risk protocol

With OpenAI Data Sharing enabled, the app can send new text from chat or First Meeting to an OpenAI safety-classification service through our servers. It checks for a signal of self-harm intent. This is a separate automated check alongside the ordinary AI response.

When that check returns a positive signal, the app presents a dismissible support card with options to call or text a U.S. crisis-support service or open its website. The user decides whether to contact that service. Choosing a contact option takes place through the phone or external service and is not a call made by the character.

The app does not automatically send a crisis report to a human, contact emergency services, or alert a family member. Its support inbox and content-report system are not live crisis-monitoring services.

Important limits

  • The classification is automated and can miss indirect or ambiguous expressions of distress, or flag something incorrectly.
  • The current check is text-based. A photo sent without text does not receive this particular risk check. Dictation participates when its transcript is sent as text.
  • The check requires AI data-sharing permission and a working service connection. Network or provider failures can prevent a support card from appearing.
  • Ordinary replies may be processed in parallel with the check. The current check does not by itself guarantee that every harmful AI response is blocked.
  • There is no automatic human intervention or guarantee that someone is reviewing a conversation. The app is not a medical, therapy or emergency-response service.

If there is immediate danger, use local emergency services or seek help from a qualified person directly. Do not rely on the app to detect a crisis or obtain help for you.

Content boundaries

The public app is intended for non-explicit interactions. Photo-planning instructions decline unsuitable or suggestive scenes, and image generation also uses the AI provider’s content safeguards. Text and attached-image processing rely on the model and provider’s safeguards. These measures can reject requests, but they cannot guarantee that every inappropriate input or output will be blocked.

Reports and privacy

You can report a selected AI response or image in the app. That sends the selected content and any optional comment to the developer for review. It is separate from the automated check and should not be used to obtain urgent help.

Read the Privacy Policy for the information sent to OpenAI, reports sent through our email provider, and how to withdraw AI data-sharing permission. For questions about this protocol, use our support form.
