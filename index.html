Nora likes having plans to look forward to, but thinks the best ones leave room to change your mind. You might agree, tease her about it, or tell her what you’re planning. A simple hello can lead to the ideas and values that matter to you.

Begin with what
matters to you

Tell First Meeting about your interests, your values and the kind of connection you’d like. You can share the pace and tone you prefer, including whether you enjoy a little playful debate.

Follow-up questions help shape her personality and the things you have in common. You’ll get to read her introduction and choose her portrait.

A world
of her own.

Nora wants to find a quieter path to the coast. She’s tempted by a route she hasn’t tried before, leaving room for a surprise. In a later conversation, she might tell you which way she chose.

Her fictional world develops through your conversations, giving her stories of her own to share.

Photos from the conversation

An AI-generated photo can bring a moment from that world into view. You can ask for a photo, or one may appear during a chat. The scene and your companion’s chosen appearance guide the image. Details can vary.

Pick up a thread

You mentioned a friend coming to visit. Another day, she might ask how the visit went.

Selected details from earlier chats and saved memories can inform later replies. You can review and edit saved memories, though your companion may still miss or misremember a detail.

Common questions

Who am I chatting with?

An AI-generated character with a personality and a fictional world. First Meeting helps shape her around what you share, and her story develops through your conversations. Nora and the dialogue on this page are illustrative examples.

Can I choose a different kind of companion?

Yes. Tell First Meeting about the companion and connection you want. Men’s and women’s appearances are available, and you can choose friendship or romance. You can share the pace and tone you prefer. Romance is non-explicit.

How will access work?

Replies, photos and dictation will use credits. Paid subscriptions will include a credit allowance, with separate top-ups available. Prices and allowances will be shown before purchase.

How is my information processed?

Your chat history and saved memories are stored on your device. With your permission, selected content is sent through our cloud services to OpenAI for AI features. An encrypted iCloud backup is optional. Analytics, purchases and support have separate data flows. Read the Privacy Policy for details and your controls.
