Common questions

Who am I chatting with?

An AI-generated character with a personality and a fictional world. First Meeting helps shape her around what you share, and her story develops through your conversations. Nora and the dialogue on this page are illustrative examples.

Can I choose a different kind of companion?

Yes. Tell First Meeting about the companion and connection you want. Men’s and women’s appearances are available, and you can choose friendship or romance. You can share the pace and tone you prefer. Romance is non-explicit.

How will access work?

Replies, photos and dictation will use credits. Paid subscriptions will include a credit allowance, with separate top-ups available. Prices and allowances will be shown before purchase.

How is my information processed?

Your chat history and saved memories are stored on your device. With your permission, selected content is sent through our cloud services to OpenAI for AI features. An encrypted iCloud backup is optional. Analytics, purchases and support have separate data flows. Read the Privacy Policy for details and your controls.
