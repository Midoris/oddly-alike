Support

Questions about Oddly Alike, your data or a purchase? Get in touch.

Contact the developer

Send a message to Ievgenii Iablonskyi, the developer of Oddly Alike. We’ll reply to the email you provide. For a technical issue, include your app version, iOS version and a brief description.

10–4,000 characters. Please don’t include passwords, payment-card details or recovery keys.

Your email and message are processed by our support backend and Resend to deliver your request. Read the Privacy Policy. This form is not an emergency service.

You can also use Contact Support in the app’s Settings to prepare an email with an editable system report. Review that report and remove anything you do not want to send.

Manage AI data sharing

Open Settings → My Data → OpenAI Data Sharing in the app. Turning it off pauses AI chat, memory processing, dictation and photo generation. You can still read saved conversations. This permission does not control Firebase Analytics, wallet records or messages you send to support.

See the AI processing section of the Privacy Policy for what is shared and why.

Delete your workspace or wallet

  • Workspace and backup: choose Delete All My Data in Settings and confirm. Keep the app available while any cloud deletion is pending.
  • Wallet account: if signed in with Apple for the wallet, use its separate Delete Wallet Account control in Settings. Closing the wallet does not erase your device workspace.
  • Other records: use the form above to ask about guest, service-side or support records you want reviewed for deletion. We may need to verify the request, and some purchase, security or legally required records may remain.

These actions do not cancel an Apple subscription. The Privacy Policy’s deletion section explains the separate steps and limits.

Subscriptions and purchase issues

Manage or cancel a subscription through your Apple Account. Apple explains how to cancel a subscription. For refund requests, use Apple’s Report a Problem service. Use the form above if a verified purchase or credit balance is not appearing correctly in the app.

Report an AI response or photo

Use the reporting action associated with that response or image in the app. A report sends the selected AI content and any comment you add to the developer for review. The Privacy Policy describes these reports and support communications. Read AI and safety for the current safety features and their limitations.
